Policies

Return & Refund Policy

At Seyon Touch, we carefully inspect and securely pack every jewellery order before dispatch.

No COD

We currently do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD). All orders must be prepaid.

No Refunds for Change of Mind

We do not accept returns or refunds for change of mind, incorrect product selection, or personal preference.

Unboxing Video Is Mandatory

For your protection, please record a clear, continuous unboxing video while opening your package. The video should clearly show the package, shipping label, and the jewellery inside.

Without a valid unboxing video, damage or missing-item claims may not be accepted.

Damaged Product

If your jewellery arrives damaged, please contact us with the clear unboxing video as proof.

After verification and approval, the eligible refund amount will be processed within 3 working days.

Please do not discard the packaging until your order has been fully checked.
Your trust matters to us, and we are committed to ensuring a safe and pleasant shopping experience.
